A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Sunday 22 November
2026, built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122)
bounty "Sum up 22 November's world news in one image".

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network, fully deterministic)
draws the front page and writes it to `/output/entry.png` (1024×1024,
byte-identical on every run). Every letter is drawn from a small 5×7 bitmap
font, and the PNG bytes are encoded by hand.

Lead story: COP31 nears a deal at dawn in Antalya after two days of
overtime. Also covered: Eisenkot tapped to form Israel's next government,
the Riemann proof questioned a day after its Lean certification, Hong Kong's
Election Committee vote, the magnitude 4.1 quake in Myanmar, a fourth
weekend of protests in France, the Alcaraz–Zverev ATP Finals decider, plus
the Peru quake toll and Zelensky on NATO membership.
